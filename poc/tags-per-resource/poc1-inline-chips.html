POC 1 — “inline chips, no magic”. Tags stay written exactly as today (trailing backticks per entry in the README), but instead of being stripped from the entry and merged into the page footer, each token renders as a small clickable chip inside its own resource line. The Tags index becomes resource-level: clicking a chip jumps to that tag's section, which lists the tagged resources across the whole site, not the pages. No JavaScript needed — pure anchor links. (OSINT/theHarvester/SpiderFoot entries: 9Ghz and HIBP are real catalog data; two OSINT entries are sample fill with plausible badges to make the list realistic.)
